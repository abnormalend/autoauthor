## Framing

- form_noun — "short story"
- unit_noun — "scene"
- reader_persona — a reader who will finish this in twenty minutes and has
  read four hundred others; they are not owed a world, they are owed one
  thing done exactly

The SFWA boundary. Below 1,000 words is flash, which this pipeline does
not serve — five phases cost more than the story does. Above 7,500 the
apparatus a novelette needs starts to earn itself and `novella` takes over.

The gate is lower than a novel's on purpose, and it is not a lower
standard. The foundation bar is the highest in the pipeline because a weak
plan costs the drafting loop far more than it costs to plan again — true
across eighty thousand words, false across five, where the drafting loop is
cheap enough to absorb the error and the plan cannot be much righter than
the story.

## Form Contract

- The story has ONE central effect. Not one theme and one twist and one
  character study — one thing the reader is left holding.
- It does not end on the first beat of a novel it is not going to write.
  An ending that gestures at the larger story is a breach, however
  elegantly it gestures.
- Nothing is established that the story does not use. At this length,
  setup that never pays is the most expensive mistake available.

## Drafting Rules

25. There is no room to establish and then use. Establish BY using: the
    rule, the relationship, the wound all arrive inside an action that
    also advances the story.
26. Enter each scene as late as it can be entered and leave before the
    reaction is complete. A short story has no transitions to spend.
27. One POV, one timeframe, unless the story's whole effect depends on
    breaking that — and if it does, say so in the plan.

## Base Dimensions

- single_effect — Does everything in the story serve one effect? Name it
  in a sentence. Then take each scene and say what it contributes to that
  sentence; a scene that contributes something ELSE, however good, is a
  second story sharing the wordcount. If two or more scenes serve a
  different effect, score 6 max. If you cannot name the effect from the
  plan at all, score 4 max.
- compression — Is the sentence-level writing doing more than one job at a
  time? Test three passages: does each simultaneously characterize and
  advance, or describe and reveal? Prose that is merely efficient is not
  compressed — compression is density, not brevity. If the plan's exemplar
  passages read as a novel's opening chapter at reduced length, score 6
  max.

## Foundation Guidance

Three layers, not seven. `world.md` is the layer that most obviously
cannot be earned here — a five-thousand-word story cannot spend eight
hundred words orienting a reader — so what world there is lives inside
`characters.md` and the outline, as the specific facts the story touches
and no others.

- `voice` — a full voice document. Voice matters MORE here, not
  less: at this length it is most of what the reader experiences, and
  there is no plot architecture to carry a flat one.
- `characters` — the protagonist in depth, and only the characters who
  appear. A wound/want/need/lie chain is still wanted for the
  protagonist; for anyone else, what they want in the scene they are in.
- `outline` — scene by scene rather than chapter by chapter, in the
  genre pack's beat vocabulary, compressed to the beats that fit.

`layers` is what foundation builds. Drafting may still write a `canon.md`
— facts established on the page need recording, and in a collection the
shared bible is fed from below — and that is not the same as planning one
in advance, which at this length is waste.

Dropped, and why: `foreshadowing_balance` scores a tracked ledger, and a
story that plants and pays within four pages does not keep one — the plants
are visible in the outline itself. `canon_coverage` guards against a
contradiction introduced in chapter 5, and there is no chapter 5.
`character_secrets` asks for a secret whose revelation changes the plot's
trajectory, which is a novel's mechanism; at this length a secret is either
the story's single effect or it is clutter, and `single_effect` scores it
better.
